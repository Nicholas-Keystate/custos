# Companions — roadmap

The kernel travels alone until ratification; companion documents
join here afterward, each after an update pass bringing it to
commensurability with the 4.0 kernel's vocabulary and rulings.
None is normative until it says so itself.

| Companion | Subject | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **GLEIF EGF mapping** | The vLEI Ecosystem Governance Framework read through Custos objects — root AID as gAID, framework corpus as Constitution, QVI qualification as seating, requalification as cadence, revocation as recourse. Thesis by exhibit: the world's largest operating governance framework, mapped at honest grades (exhibited-in-production vs Custos-adds). | Seeded; 4.0 update pass pending |
| **Philosophy** | Why a governance event log at all: the elimination ladder (why no per-artifact mechanism suffices), the fold (log = evidence, Constitution = computed judgment), succession-by-accountability, and the standard's epistemology (checks that can fail). | Seeded; 4.0 update pass pending |
| **Confidentiality & anchored delivery** | Two sides of one boundary. Disclosure: salt/assurance grades, blinding limits, what a domain owes and refuses. Delivery: the anchored content graph — a domain's serving surface is the digest-closure of its committed anchors; what is committed is owed, what is unanchored is not. The corpus shape is the substrate's own (SAID-addressed content, typed edges); what Custos adds is the anchoring discipline, never the shape. | Seeded; 4.0 update pass pending |
