# Companions — roadmap

Three companions travel beside the ratified kernel. All are
informative: the kernel rules on any divergence, and nothing
here is normative for Custos or anyone else.

| Companion | Subject | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [**GLEIF EGF mapping**](gleif-egf-mapping.md) | The vLEI Ecosystem Governance Framework read through Custos objects — root AID as gAID, framework corpus as Constitution, QVI qualification as seating, requalification as cadence, revocation as recourse. Thesis by exhibit: the world's largest operating governance framework, mapped at honest grades (exhibited-in-production vs Custos-adds). | Minted against the ratified 4.0 kernel |
| [**Philosophy**](philosophy.md) | Why a governance event log at all: the elimination ladder (why no per-artifact mechanism suffices), the fold (log = evidence, Constitution = computed judgment), succession-by-accountability, and the standard's epistemology (checks that can fail). | Minted against the ratified 4.0 kernel |
| [**Confidentiality & anchored delivery**](confidentiality-and-anchored-delivery.md) | Two sides of one boundary. Disclosure: salt/assurance grades, blinding limits, what a domain owes and refuses. Delivery: the anchored content graph — a domain's serving surface is the digest-closure of its committed anchors; what is committed is owed, what is unanchored is not. The corpus shape is the substrate's own (SAID-addressed content, typed edges); what Custos adds is the anchoring discipline, never the shape. | Minted against the ratified 4.0 kernel |
| [**The verifiable dossier as a committed gluing**](dossier-as-path-of-gluings.md) | A verifiable dossier (ToIP KSWG, Hardman/Provenant) is a *proposed gluing*: a curated graph of splices — ACDC edges, bridge wrappers, observation attestations, joint issuances, annotation chains — assembling facts no single world decided into one account; verification-by-traversal is a composition check that today rests on an unstated tree assumption. Cross-recognition creates cycles where every splice is honest and the triangle still fails — the loop test (replay the cycle, byte-compare at a log coordinate) is the trial balance federations do not yet keep, and its findings are observation attestations. Four MAY-grade thoughts for digestion. | Draft v0.2 against the ratified 4.1 kernel |
