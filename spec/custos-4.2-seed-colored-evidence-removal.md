## What this seed carries

One object form leaves section 6, and no doctrine leaves with it.

R16 rules removal, and rules it as **identification** rather than
as retreat: the content colored evidence was to carry is already
carried by an object the document types, and the one component
that was undefined turns out to be a thing R13 has now named.
Nothing is lost, which is why nothing has to be designed to
replace it.

## Repair — the removal

**Ratified spans.** Cited, not edited. The claim (L842–847):

> What crosses a frame boundary is never judgment; it is
> committed evidence in portable form. Four object forms carry
> every crossing this document itself makes. They are this
> kernel's forms, not a proven closure of the crossing space, and
> they interrelate rather than partition — a warranty may travel
> as a component of colored evidence, and a cone is a collection
> over the others:

The fourth form (L886–896):

> - **Colored evidence** — evidence traveling with the committed
>   inputs by which a frame's judgment colors it … The object
>   carries its color-computation inputs: the colorless base
>   (valid to every governance-blind consumer), a committed view
>   echo, and a lens citation; optionally a claimed color as
>   warranty.

And the specifies-list's fourth item (L449–451):

> - the medium and the objects that cross it — the
>   frame-invariant substrate, and the portable forms (edict,
>   verification cone, warranty, colored evidence) every crossing
>   uses;

**The defect.** The term occurs twice as an unbroken string in
2471 lines — L451 and L886 — and once more split across a line
break at L846–847. No normative span requires producing,
consuming, or verifying it. Section 6's object-typing clause
(L898–908) types the edict, the warranty, and requirement
elements; it types neither colored evidence nor the cone. Its
component "a committed view echo" (L892) occurs exactly once in
the document and is defined nowhere.

So an implementer told the crossing space is carried by four
object forms must build a form whose components are undefined,
whose typing clause omits it, and for which no fixture, vector,
or consuming clause exists. Section 1.7 requires every construct
to be introduced as a named composition of the seven primitives,
with the introducing section stating the composition in its own
prose (L396–398), and the introduction states the consequence:
"a construct that cannot state its composition is a defect by
this document's own rule" (L52–53).

**Replacement.**

> **Three object forms carry every crossing this document itself
> makes** — edict, verification cone, warranty. They are this
> kernel's forms, not a proven closure of the crossing space, and
> they interrelate rather than partition: a cone is a collection
> over the others, and a warranty travels with the evidence it
> is about.
>
> A crossing that requires transporting a colorless base, a
> committed view echo, and a lens citation as a single object
> reinstates a fourth form, at the position where such a crossing
> is exhibited. None is exhibited in this document.

Section 2's list drops its fourth portable form with it, and
becomes true at its fourth item.

## Ground — what already carries the content

Colored evidence was to travel with three things. Each is
already carried, and by one composition the document names.

- **The colorless base** is the evidence object itself. Section
  12.3 states the property that made the base worth naming
  (L1710–1713): "The colorless base remains valid to every
  consumer that ignores governance entirely: a validator that
  knows nothing of GARDs parses and verifies the same bytes
  unharmed." That is a property of ordinary committed evidence,
  not a component that has to be assembled into a container.
- **The lens citation** is the warranty's own pinned lens.
  Section 6 types it: a warranty is "emitted under a pinned lens"
  (L879–880), and the typing clause requires "its lens cited by
  edge" (L901–904). Section 4 defines what a lens is and what a
  pinned lens is (L752–755).
- **The claimed color as warranty** was already optional and
  already a warranty.

The composition of the three has a name in the ratified text.
Section 17's compact-form gate calls it a **warranted receipt**
(L2274–2284) — "the wire shape by which a warrantor's backing
travels per-receipt" — and rules that until the gate's ordered
conditions stand, warranted receipts travel in the heavyweight
envelope form, "which is complete and lawful today". So the
crossing colored evidence was to serve is already served, today,
by an object with a committed wire posture and a gated
improvement path.

## Ground — the undefined component was a thing after all

"A committed view echo" was the one genuinely undesigned piece,
and R16 identifies it: it is the finding's **birth-committed
declaration** — the citation enumeration of the evidence bundle
together with the discharge record — which R13 names in order to
make pertinence decidable.

That identification is what turns this from a deletion into an
identification. The component was not vacuous; it was the same
object the bearing ruling had to name for its own reasons, seen
from the carriage side and never connected to the appraisal side.
Once connected, it needs no separate definition and no separate
transport: a finding already declares what it stands on at birth,
and a warranty over that finding already cites it.

## What does not move

The participial doctrine stays whole. Section 1.6 types color as
the domain's fold in force and rules that evidence does not have
a color of its own (L361–363); section 12.2's step 4 leaves
conferral to the consuming frame (L1657–1661); section 12.3 rules
color computed and never asserted (L1701–1709); section 13.2's
commons rung carries "colorless in the commons, colored by
whoever adopts a lens" (L1867–1870). Not one of those sentences
depends on the object form. What is removed is a **container**,
and the doctrine it was going to carry is carried by the document
in five other places.

That distinction is the whole of the ruling's word
"identification": the object is not abandoned as a bad idea. It
is recognized as a name for a composition the document has.

## Reinstatement on exhibited need

The clause above keeps the door open exactly as the docket
drafted it, and the standing is deliberate. The steelman for
defining rather than removing is real: naming the object makes
the participial doctrine concrete and pre-commits a wire shape
that a later multi-lens or confidentiality profile will want. It
is an argument for defining the object *when a consumer exists*,
and not an argument for listing it undefined. When such a profile
exhibits the crossing, the fourth form returns with an emitter, a
consumer, and a fixture, which is the state the other three are
in today.

## What this unblocks

Finding #37's comprehension-gate pass. Half of it was blocked
because the drafting pass cannot state colored evidence's
composition from the seven primitives while its components are
undefined. With the form removed, section 6's remaining three
forms each have a statable composition, and the gate pass
proceeds without waiting on a design nobody has.

Section 2's "This document specifies" list stops being false at
its fourth item, which is a section 15 concern as much as a
section 6 one: prose that implies design beyond the boundary is a
defect of that prose by the openness clause's own keyword force
(L2047–2050).

## Notes for the drafting authority

Things surfaced in drafting that R16 did not name.

1. **The cone is also untyped, and this repair does not fix
   it.** Finding #38 observes that section 6's object-typing
   clause types the edict, the warranty and requirement elements
   — and neither colored evidence *nor the cone*. Removing the
   fourth form leaves three, one of which is still untyped. The
   cone is in a materially better position than colored evidence
   was — it has a normative closure rule (L872–877), a consuming
   clause in section 12.2, and a named carriage form — so this is
   not the same defect. But a drafting pass that says "three
   object forms" should check whether the typing clause is now
   complete over them, and it is not.

2. **The count sentence is load-bearing prose and appears
   twice.** Section 6's "Four object forms carry every crossing"
   and section 2's parenthetical list are the two sites, and they
   must move together or the document contradicts itself in the
   same edition. The appendix of record accounts both as one
   delta.

3. **"Warranted receipt" is used once and is not defined.**
   Section 17 uses the term at L2282 as though it were
   established, and no earlier section establishes it. If the
   removal above leans on the composition — as this seed's ground
   does — then the composition deserves naming where the objects
   are introduced, or section 17's use stays orphaned. This is a
   pre-existing gap that the repair makes more visible rather
   than one it creates, and it is the kind of thing #37's gate
   pass should catch.

4. **No station obligation is assigned, and none is owed.** The
   sitting assigns conformance vectors to R13, R15, R14, R20,
   R17 and 11a, and not to R16 — correctly, since a removal
   discharges no new behavior. The vector that *would* have been
   owed is the one that could never be written: a fixture for an
   object with no emitter and no consumer. Recorded because the
   absence is evidence for the ruling rather than an oversight.
