## S6-1 — the object and its three principles

The Constitution of a governed domain is a committed object, emergent from the Governance Event Log's activity. Its architecture is based in three principles:

- **(a) Bind-don't-mint.** The Constitution is a typed ACDC.
  Clause blocks are attribute sub-blocks. The aggregate head is
  ACDC's selectively disclosable aggregate, taken by citation —
  not a Custos-minted combinator. The head is derived state:
  any holder deterministically recomputes it from the clause-set-in-force. Custos
  retains the semantics stratum (admissible clause forms, the
  canonical clause form, what the aggregate means as law, when
  it binds, the chain-link posture for admitted disclosure).
  KERI binds the structure of the constitution, CESR determines how the carriage is composed, ACDC answers how the head is computed and disclosed;
  Custos answers what the head means and when it governs. The
  binding is pinned through the engagement companion (present as
  of sn 193). This closes GF-4 — the head function is
  determined, by citation — and discharges the selective-
  disclosure debt through the mechanism's own salts.
- **(b) Promoted ceremony grammar.** The stewardship's own
  ceremony artifacts type the constitutional life-cycle,
  column for column: succession object → amendment object;
  evidence manifest → deliberation record; ratification and
  effectiveness acts → enactment; lineage record →
  law-in-force. Five successions of byte-precedent (sn 170–193)
  ground the promotion. The standard types what its stewardship
  already does; nothing is invented.
- **(c) Office-temporality.** The knot designates by position.
  Editions are occupancies. Law-in-force is a fold-derivable
  function of position. The cited-law-head field (PR #72's
  corpus) gains its determined referent.

The principles compose: (a) gives the object its bytes, (b) its
life-cycle grammar, (c) its temporality. Each alone is partial;
the ruling is the composition.

## S6-2 — order-as-law

By default, clause order carries no consequence. Conflicts
resolve by an explicit rule committed in the clauses. Where order
is consequential, the ordering is itself committed clause
content — the covenant logic sequence, and any sequence it
references, is the precedence commitment. In both cases the
aggregate's canonical order is carriage only. Legal order never
rides on carriage order; where legal order exists, it is in the
bytes. This is the no-ambient law executed at the clause layer,
and it removes the order objection to principle (a).

## S6-3 — covenant inconsistency: conviction by the domain's own voice

A self-inconsistent covenant set is convictable by proof. The
proof object cites the conflicting clause blocks by SAID
(selective disclosure admits proving the conflict while
disclosing only the conflicting clauses) and derives the
contradiction under the committed covenant logic — the covenant
set's own rules deriving falsum, never an observer's opinion. A
witness or watcher carries the proof; anyone holding the head
checks it. The finding lands in `self-convicted(proof)`.

This extends duplicity detection to the covenant layer: the
domain speaking against itself in its own committed voice. The
proof grammar is Custos-minted (it is semantics: what counts as
the covenant logic deriving falsum). Each proof is a binding
(clause citations plus a derivation).

**Confessed bound.** Provable inconsistency is a subset of
inconsistency. A covenant logic rich enough to be interesting
admits inconsistencies with no finite derivation; those never
convict. The bound is graded, not closed: a governance engine
that ships a decidable covenant-logic fragment gives total
inconsistency decision at enactment time for covenant sets
authored inside the fragment. Covenant sets authored outside
keep the confessed semi-decidable posture. Engine-checked buys
total; hand-rolled keeps watcher-carried. The spec text at 4.3
carries this confession at the clause pattern's definition site.

## S6-4 — citation temperature: two committed forms, both cold

On the record there are no hot citations. A committed citation
rides an event; the event has a position; the citation's epoch is
derivable. Hotness exists only off-record, in a reader asking
what law is now — an evaluation at an uncommitted coordinate.

Two citation forms are typed, and the cited-law-head field
carries which:

- **Position-implicit** — "the law, at my position." The epoch
  derives from the citing event's own sn via the fold. The
  ordinary, contemporaneous form; the ruled subject's
  experience of law.
- **Epoch-explicit** — "the law, at cited epoch k." The epoch is
  committed in the citation. The retrospective form; the
  ruler's citation of the law in force when an appraised act
  occurred.

Ex-post-facto discipline follows as a citation-type check: an
appraisal that cites epoch-explicit law later than the appraised
act's position is convictable by inspection. It costs one
committed field.

## S6-5 — the process envelope

Process between ruled and ruler travels in a SPAC/ESSR envelope,
by citation (bind-don't-mint at the transport layer): encrypt-to-
receiver with sender attribution sealed inside the ciphertext;
the receiver named inside the signed payload; KRAM freshness on
request legs. Governance adds three commitments:

- **The process/outcome seam.** The envelope carries process; the
  fold carries outcome. Nothing binds the domain from inside a
  channel. The channel is never a referent.
- **Legal-basis citation.** The signed payload names the clause
  it travels under. Process-of-law is distinguishable from
  correspondence by committed field.
- **Office-addressed ESSR.** The receiver slot may name a
  position; the occupant derives at send time via the fold.
  Service on the office survives transfer; during vacancy it
  resolves to the caretaker; the anti-forwarding protection
  binds to the office, so process served on the office cannot be
  replayed against the occupant as a private party, nor the
  reverse.

**The delivery ladder.** Where a covenant commits an
answer-obligation, delivery has a proof ladder, each rung typed,
no rung overclaiming:

1. **Acknowledged** — a receiver-signed receipt citing the
   request's SAID and datetime: delivery into the receiver's
   signing authority at a time.
2. **Constructive** — witnessed publication to the receiver's
   own witness set plus an expired committed window: the content
   was addressable at the receiver's committed availability
   surface; the silence is now the fact. The witness set is the
   subject's committed address for process.
3. **Convictable silence** — committed window, neither rung
   above: a typed breach, landing in the codomain, never an
   ambient assumption of knowledge.

No rung claims proof of reading; the ladder proves everything
short of comprehension and confesses the remainder. A governed
KRAM sequence — monotonic datetimes, receipted both ways — is a
committed correspondence log: duplicity detection extends to
correspondence itself. A party who acknowledged at a datetime and
pleads ignorance elsewhere is convicted by their own receipts.

## S6-6 — the vacancy joints

Two offices, orthogonal temporalities: the steward's seat (the
gAID's key-state occupancy; supplement 5 governs its recovery)
and the edition's seat (which Constitution edition is law;
principle (c) governs its succession). Conflating them was the
joint's difficulty; separating them resolves it:

- **Law persists.** During `pending(under-recovery, H)`,
  law-in-force returns the last grounded edition — never
  bottom. This is a security property: were law to evaporate
  with disputed keys, forcing a recovery would be a
  lawlessness-amplification attack. The mast holds while the
  crew is disputed. New bindings during the window carry the
  typed pending standing; standing law stands.
- **Enactment during vacancy is refused by position.** An
  enactment event must carry positive evidence of seated
  occupancy in the event (the rider law). During the window it
  cannot; it is never law, convictable by inspection. The act
  is tainted, the content is not: a seated successor may
  re-enact identical bytes through a fresh ceremony.
- **Conviction lands; cure waits.** A carried inconsistency
  proof (S6-3) is observer-derivable and lands with the
  steward's seat empty. The caretaker records and acknowledges
  (maintains, never amends). The cure — an amending enactment —
  waits for a seated occupant. Per-clause salts make the
  conviction clause-scoped, not edition-fatal: the cited
  clauses return the conviction; the rest of the covenant set
  remains law.
- **The continuity authority ends vacancies, never amends.**
  The only lawful act about the office during vacancy is the
  act that ends the vacancy.
- **In-flight amendments freeze.** An amendment ratified but
  not yet effective when recovery begins awaits the seated
  successor, who completes or declines it. This requires the
  Pending and Declined slots to be distinct — the motivating
  case for issue #73's repair, cross-cited here.
- **Horizon expiry.** A domain that closes its horizon
  unregrounded is convicted per supplement 5. The record stays
  cold and replayable; every historical appraisal verifies
  forever; the archive outlives the office.

## S6-7 — the obligation stratum, both orientations

A governed domain's conduct obligations over committed content
form one stratum with two orientations, both attached to office,
both fold-recorded, both graded by conviction path:

- **Answer-obligations (availability).** A covenant names a
  content set C by SAID and commits: a well-formed, KRAM-fresh
  request for a member of C receives a signed response within a
  committed window, under committed rate and schedule bounds.
  Each response is a warranted receipt of availability at a
  time. Each failure is a typed breach. The availability of C
  over a committed sampling schedule is a fold-derivable
  function of receipts — computable by anyone, in engineering
  units. Confessed bounds: the metric is a sampled estimator
  (the schedule must be committed or the metric smuggles
  ambient order); availability is proven per-challenger, so the
  metric's strength is the committed diversity of its
  challenger set; the answer-obligation is a flood surface, and
  the KRAM windows that stop replay also bound the challenge
  burden. Maintaining C answerable is the paradigm caretaker
  obligation during vacancy.
- **Refrain-obligations (loyalty).** The duty of data loyalty
  (Richards & Hartzog; enacted in Utah S.B. 275, 1st Sub., 2026
  General Session, creating a duty of loyalty over identity-
  attribute processing at §63A-20-701, bound to the roles of
  the identity ecosystem) decomposes on the mechanical/
  appraisal grading exactly:
  - Its subsidiary rules (purpose limitation, retention
    windows, secondary-use bans, no re-presentation, notice
    before processing) enter as mechanical clauses — conduct
    checkable over committed objects. Breach is
    watcher-provable: a receipt showing processing outside the
    admitted purpose is duplicity at the loyalty layer. The
    chain-link admitted posture is the carrier: loyalty
    subsidiary rules are the content of the admitted-disclosure
    terms the flagship ruling already paired with the
    aggregate.
  - Its general standard (best interests, non-exploitation, no
    disproportionate risk) is constitutively evaluative and
    enters as committed evaluation points: the domain commits
    that the appraisal exists, which organ is seated to
    appraise, and which codomain the appraisal lands in. A
    watcher cannot derive exploitation from bytes; claiming
    otherwise would claim away what makes loyalty a standard.

The two orientations are one surface: the office must answer for
the content and refrain with the content. Duty attaches to the
position; occupancy determines who discharges it.

**Confessed asymmetry.** A statute binds the unwilling within its
jurisdiction; a covenant binds those who enter the domain. The
embedded form is the statute's own technological-means preference
discharged for the subset that opts into governed infrastructure
— never a substitute for the statute. Pins: the statutory
instance at `external-specs/utah-sb275s01-2026.pdf`, sha256
`5477417c4ba3b41d0417dc2c5e140b0959d3ff4beac5c60a187af62d2f7cf7f6`;
the doctrinal citations (Richards & Hartzog) are marked owed to
