## S3-3 — Constitution as aggregate over per-clause blinded
## sub-blocks (the flagship; repairs the disclosure postures)

Both legs independently derived the same construction — the
strongest convergence class this program's method produces.

FINDING: clause-selective disclosure as drafted proves the
WRONG thing — revealing a cited clause proves the clause was
committed, not that undisclosed evidence satisfies it, and a
verifier cannot check a revealed clause's membership in the
anchored Constitution without seeing the rest. A monolithic
SAID does not admit partial verification.

RULED (structure as law; carriage to the encoding round): the
committed Constitution's clause-set head SHALL be an aggregate
commitment over per-clause sub-blocks, each independently
SAID-addressed and carrying a per-clause blinding factor in
ACDC's u-field discipline. Clause-selective disclosure then
reveals the ground-cited clauses with their blinding factors;
the verifier recomputes membership in the anchored aggregate;
undisclosed clauses remain provably present and unreadable.
The two ratified clauses that compose into this (clause
SAID-addressing; the pre-disclosure blinding mandate) are cited
at the site. For the admitted posture: admission enactments MAY
grant disclosure under chain-link confidentiality — the law
travels as a contractually protected disclosure whose rules
carry the admission terms, so unpermitted onward disclosure is
breach with committed evidence (this materially strengthens the
narrowed-jury confession: the jury stays narrow; leaks from it
become convictable).

Because the disclosure postures live in the graduated seed
(Chapter 2), this repair enters through the seed's own station
or a gauntlet finding — never an in-place edit of graduated
bytes.

Distinct residue, ruled into the same surface: proving clause
membership is not proving satisfaction of hidden evidence. The
candidate SHALL distinguish three presentation forms — replay
presentation (disclose the evidence blocks), warranted
presentation (a registry-bound warranty citing finding, clause,
evidence commitment, and lens), proof presentation (a
cryptographic proof bound to the same commitments; admitted,

conflates them; the conflation is the defect.
