## R13 (#32) — RULED: bearing = conviction ∧ pertinence

"Bearing" decomposes into two questions with separate committed
homes, and both are decidable from the closed triple:

- **Conviction — is this pair conviction-grade at all?** Key
  tier: delegated to the substrate's superseding-recovery rules
  (§7.1, unchanged). Registry and governance tiers: §7.4's
  law-relative rule already answers it — a pair convicts only
  within frames that committed the violated predicate. The
  silence case is therefore already dispositioned: no committed
  predicate → no conviction → the pair is ordinary evidence;
  consume. The two "indistinguishable silences" the blind
  implementation reported were one silence with one ruled
  disposition; axiom 3's refusal guards law running out on the
  QUESTION, never on this gate.
- **Pertinence — does the conviction touch THIS finding?** A
  conviction bears on a finding iff the convicted party's
  artifact is cited in the finding's own birth-committed lists:
  the evidence bundle E's citation enumeration (R1's closure), or
  the discharged requirement space (R2's enumeration; for
  antinomy: a producing clause of the circuit is in the
  discharged space). Flat membership in a finite committed
  enumeration — no transitivity (E is closed by citation, so
  everything the finding leans on is in the list flatly), no
  ambient input, no domain declaration. Pertinence is DERIVED,
  never declared: domains commit predicates only, which §7.4
  already requires.
- **Role refinement:** the convict's role dispatches the edge.
  Subject-pertinence (the question's own subject convicted) fires
  the edge into `self-convicted`. Ground-pertinence (a cited
  third party convicted) fires R6's taint succession — tainted
  `pending`, species unresolved-conflict — the finding's voice is
  poisoned, not the question. The edge into `self-convicted`
  fires iff conviction ∧ subject-pertinence.

The definition enters ratified 4.2 text (it must be one spec
sentence, or engines diverge on transitivity). R10's held seed
unblocks: the antinomy constructor's admission condition is the
pertinence clause's clause-membership branch.

Candidate repair prose, offered to the drafting pass: *a finding
declares what it stands on at birth; a conviction bears exactly
when it strikes something on that declaration; both halves of the
check were committed before the question ever arose.*

## R15 (#35) — RULED: no ambient membership; designation from

   every other singleness is conferred by clause.

   registry grounds nothing — and the enforcement is placed
   where it is real: **the designation check enters the
   verification cone.** A finding's law head SHALL be derivable

6. **Fail-loud law:** law CONTENT may blind per the ratified

7. **Weight guard (kernel-grade):** recognition and receipt

## R14 (#33) — RULED: C, succession under re-discharge

1. **Destination:** committed evidence falsifying a cited defeat
   yields an ordinary succession — F(E′, L, p′) computed fresh by
   full discharge; with the defeater's enactment falsified, the
   defeat no longer discharges, and the fold returns what the
   requirement space now yields (possibly affirmed). The prior
   `defeated` stands at its coordinate forever. Reinstatement is
   not an edge; it is a new fact at a new coordinate whose bundle
   contains the undercut.
2. **Reversal condition (typed):** a succession may reverse a
   terminal value ONLY where the growth falsifies a ground the
   prior finding cites (undercut) — never on added contrary
   weight (rebuttal). Computable via R13's pertinence: the
   falsified artifact is in the prior finding's cited ground.
3. **Monotonicity repair:** L1103's "refines and never
   contradicts" re-scopes to the knowledge order — growth refines
   the RECORD (no finding erased, no citation un-happened);
   verdicts across successions may reverse under the condition
   above. This was the four-valued design's own shape (the FOUR's
   two orders); the ratified sentence overclaimed truth-order
   monotonicity for a defeasible system.
4. **The forbidden table survives, reasons sharpened:**
   "rehabilitation is an act, not a transition" remains exactly
   right for TAINT (a poisoned voice is cured only by an owned
   act — R6). The defeated→affirmed row's reason becomes: lawful
   only as succession, only by falsification of the cited
   defeat; otherwise rehabilitation remains an act. Taint-cure
   and ground-evaporation are different phenomena; the table now
   says so.
5. **Undercut named as a category, no new machinery:** the
   divergence from ASPIC+ is stated rather than hidden — Custos
   ranks defeat by class; undercuts of a cited ground act through
   the reversal condition, never through priority.

Why not A: indefeasibility mandates sustained false testimony — a
standing finding citing a ground the committed record proves
invalid, which consumers must keep acting on. The Ground Axiom
cannot hold that as a permanent state.
