Method authority: the seed-PR rule — the regeneration consumes
RULINGS, never seed bytes. Every seed is therefore an independent
execution of the same ruling the regenerated text executed, and
the comparison is of repair SUBSTANCE, never wording (the melder
types, never legislates; wording divergence is expected and
lawful). Classifications:

- **CONVERGED** — same repair substance, independently derived.
  Evidence that the ruling determines its repair.
- **DIVERGED** — a substantive difference; the grounds name which
  side the finding is against (seed misread the ruling, or the
  ratified text under-executed it). A DIVERGED-against-ratified
  entry is a candidate erratum.
- **ORTHOGONAL** — the seed carries repair content the ratified
  text lacks entirely; future-edition or erratum-docket material.
- **SUPERSEDED** — the seed's repair was re-shaped by a later
  ruling; the ruling is named.

Rulings cited from `reviews/ruling-record-2026-07-30.md` and its
three supplements (07-31, 08-01, 08-03); ratified sites located
through the 4.2 repair-census appendix (delta entries cited as
"delta N").

A note on span citations: every seed cites 4.1 line spans and
quotes 4.1 text ("Ratified span … cited, not edited"). The 4.2
regeneration renumbered sections (4.1 §7 → 4.2 §8, §15 → §16,
§16 → §17, etc.). Stale span numbers are not findings — the
