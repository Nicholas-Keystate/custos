## Executive verdict

The proposal is sound in KERI's own terms: it keeps the spine authoritative, refuses to invent a competing identifier layer, cites the substrate rather than restating it, and is unusually candid about what it has left open — none of the six findings attacks its core construction, and all six are recommend-revise, not reject. The one blocking finding is GOV-F3 (HIGH, confirmed): §7.3 rules that a finding is a pure function of exactly three committed inputs and that two evaluations SHALL be byte-identical, yet §7.1/§7.2 and §8/§12.2 dereference KERI's superseding-recovery calculus and the Dossier spec's threshold-operator semantics, whose revisions §3 pins only "in the engagement companion" — an artifact that does not exist in the repo (companions/ holds README, confidentiality, gleif-mapping, philosophy) and whose digest the ratification enactment never pins, while the dossier specification is not in §3's substrate of record at all. That is not the cross-implementation debt §14 confesses; it is intra-implementation drift over time, and the dossier's own MxQ change from a count to weighted unity is a live instance of the semantics a replay would silently flip. The remaining five are MEDIUM and cluster into two families: wire/carriage under-determination (CSR-F2's over-broad "the encoding layer is closed," which cancels the confession duty over exactly the serialization-kind and digest-derivation choices that break replay; CSR-F4's bundle preimage left open across text/binary domain and v1/v2 counter tables; CSR-F5's bare-SAD edict, which has no framable top-level form and which keripy's Serder rejects outright), and two governance-boundary items (GOV-F6's unilateral CESR genus reservation against the one table CESR requires all protocols to share identically; PRV-F3's unblinded SAIDs and digest seals over low-cardinality governance content, the classic missing-`u` grind). The single most important thing to address is GOV-F3: either pin the substrate and carriage specification digests in the ratified bytes (making semantics-version a governed object under §11's own criterion), or have the ratification enactment pin the engagement companion's digest and admit the dossier specification into §3's substrate of record — otherwise §7.3's determinism SHALL and §12.4's false-warranty discipline both rest on an unpinned dependency. CSR-F2 deserves a same-pass fix because it is the mechanism that keeps the carriage gaps invisible to conforming implementers.
