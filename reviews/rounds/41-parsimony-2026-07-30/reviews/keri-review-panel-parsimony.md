## Executive verdict

The reduction is sound in KERI's terms at its core: the GEL introduces no new anchoring pattern (it is sealed by the registry layer's own discipline), object typing defers to schema identifiers so no bespoke parser is required, and track one's registry-form reuse is the honest colorless base — the panel found no violation of KERI's authority, duplicity, or anchoring semantics. What it did find is underdetermination at exactly the seams §7.3's byte-identity SHALL ranges over. Two HIGH findings block: SPC-F2 — under track one, GEL events are byte-indistinguishable from ordinary TEL events and nothing committed says which anchored spans are the GEL, so the bootstrap commits track *placement* but never *membership*, and conforming implementations derive different Constitutions from identical bytes; and SPC-F5 — the pending→self-convicted edge fires on "new governed-status evidence," which is not a contradictory pair and therefore cannot satisfy the proof-package payload that §7.3 requires of the state it enters, driving a question irreversibly into a terminal state with a synthesized or empty ground. The single most important repair is SPC-F2: commit the GEL's registry designation (or an explicit span-selection predicate) in the inception-sealed founding law at the same grade as track choice, with a must-reject vector for an underivable designation — until then track one is a deferral rather than a reduction, and the document's own "no ambient order" axiom has no membership analogue to lean on. The MEDIUM tier concentrates on the same parsimony question one grain up (the third-log typing and track two's ilk table plus CESR genus buy nothing the equivalence-vector rule doesn't erase at the fold) and on totality gaps — the conviction-kind family, the compound-finding product under refusal, and colored evidence as a named crossing object with no emitter or stated composition — most of which trace to §1.7's comprehension gate being run in only one of the fifteen sections it governs, making that drafting pass the highest-leverage cheap fix.
