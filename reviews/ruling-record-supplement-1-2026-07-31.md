## C-1 (clarification) — R5's predicate in spec vocabulary

Two vocabulary defects in R5's full-payload enumeration, both
repaired to ratified vocabulary:

- **"Refusal class" is not spec vocabulary.** The ratified terms:
  §16 says **refusal grounds**; §9 (L1320–1327) names three
  refusal kinds by seal — digest mismatch, coordinate mismatch,
  clause violation — and rules that "a record that blurs them is
  unauditable." R5's enumeration is corrected to read: **refusal
  grounds, with the seal kind named per §9's three-kind
  discipline**. Same commitment, ratified name; nothing new.
- **Admission sets are subsumed, and the subsumption is now
  stated rather than implied.** §16's cross-implementation
  deliverable enumerates "corpus identities, admission sets,
  refusal grounds, cited law heads." R5's list carries "corpus
  identity"; the admission set — which committed events the
  substrate's own admission mechanics accepted into the corpus
  (§13's composed joint: substrate admission below, frame
  appraisal above) — is a constituent of corpus identity: two
  implementations with different admission sets cannot derive
  equal corpus identities. R5's predicate therefore requires
  admission-set equality *through* corpus-identity equality. For
  the avoidance of drafting doubt, the repair to §16 SHALL keep
  admission sets named explicitly in the enumeration, as the
  ratified text already has them.

## C-2 (clarification) — no-fold-tier-selection is a retype, not
## a new wall

R4 adjudicated R1's position-indexing and R2's discharge
discipline out of the wall list on the principle that new walls
enter through their own gauntlet, never as riders on a drift
repair. R9's "no fold-tier selection" enters the list under that
same principle correctly because it is not a new commitment: it
is the retype of an existing wall (first-seen survival, present
in §1.4's ratified list) whose stated obligation was a category
error. The commitment the old wall gestured at — the fold
consumes no order and makes no selection not derivable from
committed bytes — was already carried by axiom 4 and §17; the
retype names its evaluator-tier face. Membership was conserved:
seven walls before the retype, seven after; nothing entered that
was not already on a ratified list. A genuinely new wall — one
carrying a commitment no ratified text yet states — takes the
gauntlet road, as R4's principle requires.
