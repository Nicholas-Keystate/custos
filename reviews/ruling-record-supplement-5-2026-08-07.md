## S5-1 (axis 1 = 1a) — the two-branch covenant form

A rotation covenant of the ruled class SHALL carry two branches
as one committed pattern:

- **C.ordinary** — the covenant's predicate over establishment
  events not claiming recovery (the ruled instances: ceremony
  riders over `n`-derivation, availability floors over
  `wits`/`toad`, seat-continuity over delegator anchors, quorum
  floors over `kt`, enactment riders over `a`, cadence in
  committed coordinates).
- **C.recovery** — an establishment event claiming recovery
  SHALL satisfy three conditions:
  - **(a) medium-geometry admission.** The event IS a lawful
    superseding recovery under the medium's own rules,
    checkable against KEL bytes: attachment at an occupied
    position (same sn as a superseded event, prior digest to
    the latest establishment event), signed by the keys
    pre-rotated before the claimed compromise, within the open
    window (non-delegated: before fossilization by the next
    receipted establishment event; delegated: while the
    delegator admits, delegator anchor carried).
  - **(b) committed declaration.** A recovery declaration
    citing the compromise evidence-of-record, or a committed
    demand whose horizon stands open — the *why*, in bytes,
    position-indexed at the cure. A false declaration is its
    own exhibit.
  - **(c) regrounding horizon.** Within H committed positions
    (H per-domain, committed in the covenant; the standard
    requires *a* horizon, never a value), a regrounding act
    restores the ordinary regime.

**Standing during the bridge:** `pending(under-recovery, H)` —
a typed pending species. The office stands; the occupancy is
provisional; caretaker enumerations (committed at birth) bound
the provisional voice. **The conviction relocates:** a lawful
cure is never convicted; the conviction fires at the horizon's
close without regrounding — `defeated(failure-to-reground)` —
or at a failed condition: an event claiming recovery from
current keys by head-extension fails (a) on the KEL's own
geometry; a claim without declaration fails (b).

**Ground.** The medium half-makes this distinction: it
discriminates ordinary from superseding rotation *geometrically*
(attachment coordinates, key generation, window) and resolves
the remainder only as duplicity detection. The two-branch form
is the law-side completion: (a) consumes the half the medium
knows; (b) supplies the *why* the wire has no field for; (c)
supplies the normative horizon beside the physical window — two
clocks, both committed, neither ambient. Rejected forms:
exception-clause ("unless recovery" — a hole; the attacker
relabels; bridge standing untyped) and per-domain ad hoc
(per-domain bearing rules, killed at R13).

## S5-2 (axis 2 = 2a) — the regrounding act, both forms

The successor-regime clause MAY commit either form, and SHALL
commit which:

- **Reconstituted ceremony:** the custodial machinery (or its
  lawful successor under the caretaker clauses) re-enacts the
  ordinary covenant's regime.
- **Governed transfer:** a doubly-anchored transfer of registry
  control to the committed successor regime — the upstream
  `xfr` shape, admitted by the fold ONLY under the authorizing
  clause (no clause, no admission; fail-loud). An unauthorized
  transfer is refused; an authorized one is a governed
  enactment judged under the law in force at its position.

The transfer form designs against the upstream shape and its
vectors hold until the event ships (the R19 posture). The
registry-as-office reading is recorded: the transfer changes
the controller-occupancy, never the identifier; designation
(R15) names the position and survives lawful transfer.

## S5-3 (axis 3 = 3a) — delegated regrounding under evidence
## separation

The delegator MAY be the regrounding authority. The clause
pattern SHALL carry the separation sentence: the three legs
rest on three independent evidence classes — recovery
lawfulness on medium bytes (no testimony), the declaration on
evidence-of-record (datable, position-indexed), the regrounding
on the authority's committed enactment (watcher-observable,
exhibit-grade) — and **no single act discharges more than one
leg**. The quorum's approval decomposes into member signatures
from individually convictable identifiers. Confessed floor: a
fully conspiring authority with clean faces is beyond
byte-conviction; the separation narrows, never closes.

## S5-4 (axis 4 = 4a) — the continuity authority, typed

The role is typed and enters the clause pattern now: **the
party the founding law designates to end vacancies** — distinct
from the occupant (who holds the office) and the caretaker (who
administers within enumerated powers during vacancy; the
continuity authority ends the vacancy). Placeholder term
"continuity authority," marked pre-coinage; the settled name
lands by ordinary succession, no re-ruling.

## S5-6 — the recourse stack (recorded with the ruling)

Against a false cure that passes (a) and manufactures (b): the
bridge itself (typed pending, caretaker bounds, starvation at
the horizon — conviction without proving the theft); the
contest (the rightful holder of the same pre-rotated keys
counter-supersedes; the medium detects duplicity; the
both-lawful race is DEFERRED to the R6 machinery and recorded
here as the seam's open composition — with the note that branch
(c) makes the race asymmetric: one claimant can reground);
succession (the office succeeds past the poisoned occupancy on
the cited conviction; obligations carry with the office, taint
with the occupancy); and the ladder (§13's recourse ladder
gains the transfer rung: recourse against a captured frame is
succession, not only exit). Confessed floors: observer-
conditional protection only; the conspiring continuity
authority; mortality-by-silence — a domain that committed no
successor-regime clause ends at the horizon by its own founding
choice. The standard confesses this consequence at the clause
pattern's definition site and never mandates the choice
(covenant-sets operational-first law).
