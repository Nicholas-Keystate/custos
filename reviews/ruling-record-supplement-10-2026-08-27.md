## S10-1 — R17.1's carriage limb is superseded

R17.1 (supplement 2, 2026-08-01) ruled the covenant seal carried
as the substrate's typed seal with a reserved type value. That
carriage is superseded. The finding of record: the ruling minted
type-semantics into a substrate field that does not offer them,
and reserved into a namespace nobody stewards — the defect class
supplement 6 names, one stratum down, ruled before the record
had the vocabulary. The conviction is confessed, not argued.

**Layers 2 and 3 of R17's split stand exactly as ruled and as
ratified.** Attachment remains coordinate lookup and lineage
walk; satisfaction remains a fold returning the four-valued
finding. Everything the ratified text says the covenant seal
*is* — binding a subject to standing law, forward, at a
committed coordinate, surviving amendment — survives unaltered.
Only the carriage changes, and only in where the kind is
written.

## S10-2 — the adopted carriage

The repair seed (`spec/custos-4.3-seed-covenant-seal-carriage.md`,
PR #81) is adopted as the drafting input for the successor
edition; the drafting authority owns the wording. Its shape:

- The covenant is committed as an event of a Custos-defined type
  in the sealing domain's designated governance event log (R15
  commits which registry that is). The event names its own kind
  in its own type field, under this standard's grammar, and
  carries the clause identifiers of the sealed set.
- The seal is the substrate's ordinary source or event seal,
  taken unmodified. No substrate extension is claimed, requested,
  or required.
- The naming discipline restates one dereference out: for the
  digest and coordinate kinds the seal's own shape is the name;
  for the covenant seal the name is the type field of the
  committed event the seal resolves to. The consumer class that
  loses wire-visible discrimination is exactly the class that
  could never have evaluated the covenant — a verifier holding
  the seal alone holds no clause set, no law head, no candidate.
- §17's seal-type reservation (L3214-3219) is struck at the
  successor edition. The genus reservation stands on its own
  terms (#45); one reservation remains, with one steward and one
  recognition question.
- Admissibility carries forward verbatim: where byte equality is
  achievable the digest seal is mandatory, and a covenant seal
  over digest-sealable content is the defect.
- The covenant event's type value is not minted here; it is
  chartered to the encoding round (#57). The doctrinal
  reconciliation is recorded: the substrate says context
  modifies seal semantics; this standard commits the meaning in
  the event the seal resolves to; a committed event resolved
  through a seal is the disciplined form of what the substrate
  loosely calls context.

## S10-3 — the uniformity law (the ruling's addition)

The repair makes a sentence true across the whole record, and
the sentence is ruled as law at the successor edition:

> **A seal never names governance meaning. The committed event
> it resolves to does. One rule, all strata.**

The instances, named so the uniformity is checkable: the
covenant seal resolves to a covenant event whose type field
names its kind (this supplement); enactments, amendments, and
oath-gated acts are events of committed type in the governance
event log (supplements 6 and 9); the oath table and the
Constitution's clause set are committed content resolved through
ordinary anchors (supplements 6 and 9); the succession
commitment's regime binding is committed structure revealed and
checked, never a typed wrapper (the successor-binding surface,
#77). Seals, throughout, are the substrate's meaning-blind
anchors — construction proved by the medium, meaning committed
in the law. A future construct that writes governance meaning
into a carrier's type field, rather than into a committed
artifact the carrier resolves to, repeats the convicted defect
of this supplement's occasion.
